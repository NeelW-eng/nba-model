How picks are made

Win chance and value

Each player gets a projection for every stat from LightGBM models trained on five seasons of box scores. The models use recent form, per-minute rates, the minutes trend, starter role, pace, the opponent's defense against his position, rest and back-to-backs, blowout risk, and teammates who are out. Each projection becomes a full range of outcomes, which gives a chance of going over Underdog's line. That chance is blended with Underdog's own price for the line, moving at most 40% toward the model and less when the two disagree a lot, since a big gap usually means news the model hasn't seen. Picks are ranked by value: win chance × the side's payout multiplier.

Unit sizes

Under 55% (break-even)Pass

Capped at 0.5u for Questionable players and at 1u for players with fewer than 3 games this season or a payout above 1.3x. Units are tracked as if each pick were its own bet: a win at a standard payout returns 0.82u per unit (its share of a 3-pick entry's 6x), scaled by the pick's multiplier, and a loss costs the stake. Underdog takes picks in entries, not one at a time, so this is a way to keep score, not an exact copy of what an entry pays.

The black tick on the record bars marks 55%. "Paid rate" counts each win at its multiplier, so it is the number to compare with break-even. Payouts are Underdog's usual ones; check the app for your state.

Backtest: 2025–26 season, never seen in training

Trained on 2021–22 to 2023–24, tuned on 2024–25, tested on every 2025–26 game. Underdog doesn't publish past lines, so the test lines were rebuilt from what a bettor could see before each game (season and last-5 averages). Real Underdog lines are sharper, so live results will be lower than the hit rates here. Live picks are anchored to Underdog's own price for that reason.
